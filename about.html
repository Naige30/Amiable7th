Naige

Author of Amiable 7th

Gian Rizen A. Lacao, also known by his pen name Naige, is a third-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines–Biñan Campus. He was born on April 30, 2006, in Muntinlupa City. Although he was born in Muntinlupa City, he grew up in San Pedro, Laguna. During his preschool years, he graduated as Valedictorian from Evangelical Church San Pedro, Laguna. He then transferred to San Lorenzo School to continue his elementary education, where he graduated among the top 10 students of his batch. He continued his high school education at San Lorenzo School until Grade 10, graduating with honors. For Senior High School, he transferred to Muntinlupa Science High School, where he also graduated with honors. He then pursued his college education at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines–Biñan Campus, despite passing the entrance examination for De La Salle University.

About Amiable 7th

Amiable 7th was created out of Naige’s boredom. During his free time, he developed an interest in reading and eventually began writing his own stories. He later came across the book series by Gwy Saludes, which inspired him to create a story of his own. The story is heavily inspired by Naige’s experiences and memories from his high school days, incorporating elements of his life into the characters, events, and setting of Amiable 7th.
