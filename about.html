Author of Amiable 7th

Gian Rizen A. Lacao, also known by his pen name Naige, is a third-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at the Polytechnic University of the Philippines–Biñan Campus. He was born on April 30, 2006, in Muntinlupa City, and grew up in San Pedro, Laguna.

About Amiable 7th

Amiable 7th was created out of Naige's boredom. During his free time, he developed an interest in reading and eventually began writing his own stories. He later came across the book series by Gwy Saludes, which inspired him to create a story of his own. The story is heavily inspired by Naige's experiences and memories from his high school days, incorporating elements of his life into the characters, events, and setting of Amiable 7th.
